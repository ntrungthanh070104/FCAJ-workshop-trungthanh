# Reflection on "FCAJ x Agentic AI Build Week"

### Purpose of the Event

The hackathon was co-organized by FCAJ and JI Fund in Ho Chi Minh City. The competition provided a practical opportunity for developers to compete continuously for 24 hours to build applications integrating Agentic AI on the AWS platform.

For an attendee, this was a chance to observe the real-world product development process, from brainstorming and building system architecture to pitching in front of the judges. The event emphasized applying AI to solve specific business problems.

### List of Speakers and Judges

- Nguyen Gia Hung - Head of Solution Architect, AWS Vietnam
- Giuseppe Marazzotta - Head of Technology, AWS ASEAN

### Event Highlights

The shift in technology mindset and the opportunity of Agentic AI
Through the opening speech by Mr. Giuseppe Marazzotta, attendees listened to the changes in the technology industry. Previously, legacy systems needed quarters to update, but with current AI Agents, releases can happen every minute. Young engineers have a great advantage because they are not constrained by old mindsets and are fully capable of leading industry changes.

Practical project presentations from competing teams
The event witnessed many innovative solutions from the teams. A standout was the multi-channel AI ordering chatbot that helps users order food without switching applications. Other teams also brought impressive products such as an AI system that automatically draws AWS architecture for Solution Architects, a security camera analysis system for crowd control, and an automated money laundering detection process for banks.

Technical challenges and business problems
A common takeaway is that no matter how complex the technology, it must serve actual needs. The teams demonstrated how they overcame difficulties in deploying AI, handled AI hallucination issues, and optimized infrastructure costs on AWS.

### What I Learned

- Saw clearly how teams applied AWS architecture and Amazon Bedrock to build complete AI products.
- Realized that a good tech product must originate from solving the customer's exact pain points rather than just showing off technical skills.
- Understood more about the importance of limiting the project scope when time is limited, helping to complete the core features effectively.
- Learned the lifelong learning mindset from AWS experts to always be ready for continuous changes in the technology era.

### Application to Work

- Apply the observed Agentic AI and AWS service ideas to optimize workflows, such as continuing to research integrating Amazon Bedrock into applications to enhance user experience.
- Improve system deployment skills through CI/CD pipelines on GitLab, helping to automate software releases seamlessly.
- Focus more on designing user flows and applying black-box and white-box testing methods to ensure stable and accurate real-world applications.
- Apply project management skills on Jira to track and distribute workflows clearly among team members handling front-end, back-end, and QA roles.

### Experience During the Event

Learning from experts and competing teams
As an attendee, observing the teams from presenting their products to answering the judges' questions was a very practical experience. In-depth questions about infrastructure architecture, data security, and business models helped expand my mindset beyond pure programming.

Lessons learned
The competition showed that the 24-hour time pressure requires teamwork and highly sharp decision-making. The passionate atmosphere of the event added great motivation to continuously hone software development skills and seek opportunities to participate directly in real-world hackathon projects in the future.
